# HF Workbench — Versioning Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the planned evolution of HF Workbench from v1.0 forward.

---

## v1.1 — Visualization Upgrade
### Goals
- Height vs. TOA charts (Doublet, Loop, Skyloop)
- Loop vs. Skyloop comparison block
- Band-by-band loop behavior tables
- Geometry diagrams for square, delta, rectangle, irregular loops

### Status
Planned

---

## v1.2 — Vertical Systems Expansion
### Goals
- Modernize Vertical DX Designer
- Integrate Boost Engine into verticals
- Add radial modeling presets (ground, elevated, hybrid)
- Add soil conductivity presets and modeling

### Status
Planned

---

## v1.3 — DX Pattern Explorer
### Goals
- Azimuth pattern visualization
- Elevation pattern visualization
- Band-by-band DX reach estimation
- “DX Turbo” modeling mode

### Status
Concept phase

---

## v1.4 — Multi-Antenna Switching & Comparison
### Goals
- Side-by-side antenna comparison
- A/B switching simulation
- “Best Antenna Advisor” for time/band conditions
- Feedline switching loss modeling

### Status
Concept phase

---

## v2.0 — HF Workbench Pro
### Platform Goals
- Multi-module dashboard
- Save/load antenna profiles
- Exportable design reports (PDF/HTML)
- User presets and station favorites
- Mobile-optimized UI
- Plugin architecture for community modules

### Long-Term Vision
HF Workbench evolves into a complete RF engineering suite with:
- Real-time pattern visualization  
- Multi-antenna arrays  
- Terrain modeling  
- Propagation prediction  
- AI-assisted antenna optimization
