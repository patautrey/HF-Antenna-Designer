What This Antenna Is

The Elpa 301 is a multi-element HF wire array designed to provide directional gain on the lower HF bands. It uses several parallel wire elements spaced closely together to form a lightweight, high-performance directional antenna suitable for installations where full-size beams are not practical.

How This Antenna Is Modeled

  • Element spacing determines gain and front-to-back ratio.
  • Mutual coupling between elements is included for accuracy.
  • End effects adjust the electrical length of each element.
  • Height above ground controls takeoff angle and pattern shape.
  • Feed impedance is modeled at 50 Ω for compatibility.
