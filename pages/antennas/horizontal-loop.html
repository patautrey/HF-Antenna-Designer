What This Antenna Is

The Horizontal Loop, also known as the Skywire Loop, is a full‑wave wire loop mounted horizontally. It is well known for its low noise characteristics, excellent multiband performance when fed with ladder-line, and strong NVIS capability on the lower HF bands.

How This Antenna Is Modeled

  • Loop perimeter determines the fundamental resonant frequency.
  • Height above ground controls NVIS vs DX performance.
  • Shape has minimal effect on resonance but affects pattern symmetry.
  • Corner feedpoint provides a convenient impedance for ladder-line.
  • Ladder-line feed enables efficient multiband operation.
  • Full-wave loops exhibit low noise compared to dipoles.
