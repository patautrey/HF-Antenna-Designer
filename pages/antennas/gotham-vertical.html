What This Antenna Is

The Gotham Vertical is a compact HF vertical antenna that uses a mid‑mounted loading coil to achieve resonance on lower HF bands. Its small size makes it suitable for limited-space installations, though performance depends heavily on radial quality and coil efficiency.

How This Antenna Is Modeled

  • Loading coil inductance sets the resonant frequency.
  • Coil position determines current distribution and efficiency.
  • Ground losses are included due to the short radiator.
  • Loading coil losses are modeled explicitly.
  • Radial count and length strongly affect overall performance.
  • Radiation resistance is low and modeled with precision.
