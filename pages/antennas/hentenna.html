What This Antenna Is

The Hentenna is a rectangular wire antenna known for its simplicity and excellent performance on VHF bands. Developed in Japan, it provides strong horizontal polarization and a clean, predictable radiation pattern with moderate gain. Its unique shape makes it easy to build and tune.

How This Antenna Is Modeled

  • Height and width determine resonant frequency and pattern shape.
  • Feedpoint offset controls impedance and matching behavior.
  • Horizontal polarization is assumed for vertical mounting.
  • End effects adjust the required physical dimensions.
  • Current distribution is modeled along the entire loop.
  • Mounting height influences takeoff angle and gain.
