What This Antenna Is

The half-wave dipole is the most widely used HF antenna. It consists of two quarter-wave legs fed at the center and provides a predictable, efficient, and reliable radiation pattern. When installed at a good height, it offers excellent performance for both local and DX communication.

How This Antenna Is Modeled

  • Total length sets the resonant frequency.
  • Height above ground determines takeoff angle and pattern shape.
  • Inverted-V configuration modifies impedance and bandwidth.
  • End effects shorten the required physical length.
  • Wire diameter influences Q factor and bandwidth.
  • Bidirectional broadside pattern is calculated accurately.
