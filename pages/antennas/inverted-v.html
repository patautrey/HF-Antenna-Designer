What This Antenna Is

The Inverted‑V Dipole is a space‑efficient variation of the classic dipole. By sloping the legs downward from a central apex, it reduces the required horizontal span while maintaining strong performance across HF bands. Its forgiving tuning and broad bandwidth make it a popular choice for portable and permanent installations.

How This Antenna Is Modeled

  • Leg angle determines feed impedance and bandwidth.
  • Apex height controls takeoff angle and pattern shape.
  • End effects shorten the required physical length.
  • Lower leg angles broaden bandwidth but reduce gain.
  • Radiation pattern becomes more omnidirectional as legs slope downward.
