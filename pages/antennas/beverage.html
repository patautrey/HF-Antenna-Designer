What This Antenna Is

The Beverage antenna is a long-wire, low-noise receiving antenna used on the low HF bands. It operates as a traveling-wave antenna, providing excellent directivity and low-angle reception ideal for DX listening.

How This Antenna Is Modeled

  • Wire length determines directivity and low-angle sensitivity.
  • Height above ground affects noise performance and pattern shape.
  • Termination resistor sets the directionality and reduces reflections.
  • Ground conductivity influences efficiency and pattern stability.
  • Orientation determines the primary receiving direction.
