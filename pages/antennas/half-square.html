What This Antenna Is

The Half-Square antenna is a two-element wire array made from two vertical quarter-wave legs connected by a horizontal half-wave top wire. It produces strong low-angle radiation, making it an excellent HF DX antenna that is simple to build and easy to install between two supports.

How This Antenna Is Modeled

  • Vertical legs generate the low-angle DX radiation.
  • The horizontal top wire sets spacing and phase relationship.
  • Corner feedpoint provides a near 50-ohm match.
  • Height above ground determines takeoff angle and pattern shape.
  • Mutual coupling between legs is included for accuracy.
  • End effects adjust the electrical length of each segment.
