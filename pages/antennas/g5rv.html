What This Antenna Is

The G5RV is a multiband HF antenna created by Louis Varney (G5RV) in 1946. It uses a 102-foot dipole and a 34-foot ladder-line matching section to transform impedance before transitioning to coax. With a tuner, it provides broad HF coverage and is widely used due to its simplicity and versatility.

How This Antenna Is Modeled

  • The 34 ft ladder-line section is modeled as an impedance transformer.
  • Coax losses are included, especially on high-SWR bands.
  • Height above ground determines takeoff angle and NVIS/DX balance.
  • Horizontal, inverted-V, and sloper configurations are supported.
  • A tuner is assumed for all bands except near 20m resonance.
