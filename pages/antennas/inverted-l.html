What This Antenna Is

The Inverted‑L antenna combines a vertical radiator with a horizontal top wire, forming an “L” shape. It is widely used on the low HF bands because it offers strong low‑angle radiation for DX while fitting into modest spaces where a full vertical or dipole would not fit.

How This Antenna Is Modeled

  • Vertical section produces the primary low‑angle DX radiation.
  • Horizontal section increases electrical length and tuning range.
  • Ground losses are included and dominate low‑band efficiency.
  • Radial count and length directly influence performance.
  • Current distribution is modeled along both legs of the antenna.
  • End effects adjust the required physical wire length.
