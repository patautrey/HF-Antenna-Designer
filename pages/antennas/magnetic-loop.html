What This Antenna Is

This magnetic loop follows the high-efficiency design principles taught by John Portune. It uses large copper tubing, a high-Q tuning capacitor, and a properly sized coupling loop to achieve excellent performance in restricted spaces such as balconies, apartments, and portable setups.

How This Antenna Is Modeled

  • Loop circumference is set between 0.1λ and 0.25λ for optimal efficiency.
  • Large-diameter copper tubing reduces loss resistance and increases Q.
  • High-Q vacuum/butterfly capacitors provide narrow bandwidth and efficiency.
  • Efficiency is computed from radiation vs. loss resistance.
  • Skin effect and conductor losses are included for realistic modeling.
  • Coupling loop diameter is 20–25% of the main loop diameter.
  • Capacitor voltage is calculated and displayed for safety awareness.
