What This Antenna Is

The Halo antenna is a horizontally polarized omnidirectional loop, commonly used on VHF for mobile weak-signal operation. It is essentially a folded dipole bent into a circle, often gamma-matched for 50-ohm feed.

How This Antenna Is Modeled

  • Loop diameter determines resonant frequency and radiation pattern.
  • Conductor diameter affects bandwidth and Q factor.
  • Gamma match length controls feedpoint impedance.
  • Horizontal polarization provides better weak-signal performance.
  • Mounting height influences horizon gain and pattern symmetry.
