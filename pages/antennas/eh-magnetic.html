What This Antenna Is

The EH Magnetic Antenna uses two short conductive cylinders driven with carefully phased currents to create both electric and magnetic fields that combine to produce radiation. Its extremely small size relative to wavelength makes it attractive for HF operation in very limited spaces.

How This Antenna Is Modeled

  • Cylinder dimensions and spacing determine resonant behavior.
  • Tuning capacitance sets the required E/H field phasing.
  • Radiation resistance is modeled explicitly due to its very low value.
  • Efficiency is computed from conductor and phasing losses.
  • Mounting height influences pattern but not basic operation.
