What This Antenna Is

A ground plane antenna is a quarter-wave vertical radiator with several radials that act as a reflective ground surface. It provides omnidirectional coverage and is commonly used on HF, VHF, and UHF bands due to its simplicity and reliable performance.

How This Antenna Is Modeled

  • Radiator length sets the resonant frequency.
  • Radial angle influences feedpoint impedance and bandwidth.
  • Mutual coupling between radials is included for accuracy.
  • Radial count affects efficiency and pattern symmetry.
  • Mounting height determines takeoff angle and horizon gain.
