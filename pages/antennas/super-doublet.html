What This Antenna Is

The Super Doublet is a center-fed HF wire antenna fed with open-wire or ladder-line and matched with a balanced tuner. Unlike a resonant dipole, the Super Doublet is designed for efficient multiband operation, taking advantage of low-loss balanced feedline even at high SWR.

How This Antenna Is Modeled

  • Total wire length shapes the radiation pattern on each band.
  • Open-wire feedline minimizes loss across all HF bands.
  • Height above ground determines takeoff angle and NVIS/DX balance.
  • A balanced tuner is assumed for proper multiband matching.
  • Longer doublets develop multi-lobed patterns ideal for DX.
