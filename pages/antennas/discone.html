What This Antenna Is

The discone is an ultra-wideband antenna consisting of a disc above a cone. It provides vertical polarization, omnidirectional coverage, and bandwidth ratios from 3:1 up to 10:1 depending on geometry.

How This Antenna Is Modeled

  • Disc diameter sets the upper frequency limit.
  • Cone length and angle set the lower frequency limit.
  • Feed impedance is typically near 50 Ω.
  • Bandwidth ratio determines tuning range.
  • Mounting height affects horizon gain.
