HF Antenna Designer
Quick Start Guide
============================================================

This guide gets you productive in under 60 seconds.

Step 1 — Open the App

Open:

index.html

or your GitHub Pages deployment.

Step 2 — Choose a Category

Examples:

• Verticals
• Dipoles
• Loops
• Yagis

Step 3 — Select a Module

Click any antenna type.

Example:

Vertical Designer

Step 4 — Enter Frequency

Most modules begin with:

Frequency (MHz)

Enter your operating frequency.

Step 5 — Review Results

You’ll see:

• Element lengths
• Impedance
• SWR
• Efficiency
• Gain
• TOA
• Geometry tables

Step 6 — Export

Use Export Tools to save your design.

Step 7 — Explore Labs

Labs provide deeper analysis:

• Feedline Lab
• SWR Lab
• Pattern Lab
• Ground Lab
