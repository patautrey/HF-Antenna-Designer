# Antenna Workbench — Quick Start Guide

This guide gets you from zero to results in under two minutes.

---

## 1. The Workbench Flow

1. Pick a tool from the left navigation.
2. Enter your **frequency**.
3. Enter the **geometry** (height, length, perimeter, etc.).
4. Adjust **boost options** (ground, feedline, time of day).
5. Click **Compute**.
6. Read the **summary**:
   - Total gain (dBi)
   - Estimated TOA (degrees)
   - Geometry adjustments
   - Boost breakdown
   - Transformer notes

---

## 2. Vertical DX — Fast Recipe

1. Frequency: 14.2 MHz  
2. Height: 10 m  
3. Radials: 8 × 5 m  
4. Ground: “Average”  
5. Boost:
   - Time of day: “Day”
   - Seaside: optional
   - Ground screen: optional
   - Feedline: RG‑213, 75 ft  
6. Compute  
7. Look for:
   - Gain: 2–6 dBi depending on boosts  
   - TOA: 5–20° (DX‑friendly)

---

## 3. Vertical NVIS — Fast Recipe

1. Frequency: 7.1 MHz  
2. Height: 4–6 m  
3. Top hat: optional  
4. Ground loss: 5–20 Ω  
5. Radials: 8 × 5 m  
6. NVIS reflector: optional  
7. Boost:
   - Time of day: “Night” for 40m NVIS  
   - Ground screen: optional  
8. Compute  
9. Look for:
   - TOA: 60–90° (regional coverage)
   - Reflector gain: +1 to +3 dB

---

## 4. Doublet — Fast Recipe

1. Frequency: 7.1 MHz  
2. Total length: 40 m  
3. Height: 10 m  
4. Feedline: 450Ω ladder line, 75 ft  
5. NVIS reflector: optional  
6. Boost:
   - Seaside: optional  
   - Ground screen: optional  
7. Compute  
8. Look for:
   - Electrical length (% of λ)
   - Transformer notes (tuner requirements)

---

## 5. Skyloop — Fast Recipe

1. Frequency: 3.55 MHz  
2. Perimeter: ~1λ (≈ 280 m)  
3. Height: 5–10 m  
4. NVIS reflector: optional  
5. Boost:
   - Ground screen: optional  
6. Compute  
7. Look for:
   - Gain: 1.5–3 dBi  
   - TOA: 60–90° (NVIS)
