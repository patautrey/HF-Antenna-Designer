HF Workbench – Quick Start

1. First-Time Checklist

  1. Open HF Workbench in a modern browser (desktop recommended).
  2. Confirm you see the top navigation: Home, Doublet, Loop, Skyloop, Verticals, Help.
  3. If the page looks outdated, perform a hard refresh (Ctrl+F5 or equivalent).

2. Basic Workflow

2.1 Pick a Tool

  1. Click Doublet, Loop, Skyloop, or open Verticals ▼ and choose a vertical tool.
  2. Wait for the tool interface to appear in the main content area.

2.2 Enter Your Parameters

  • Set the band or frequency you care about.
  • Enter lengths (total length, leg length, or loop perimeter).
  • Enter height above ground and any radial or ground system details.

2.3 Read the Results

  • Look for notes on band coverage, pattern behavior, and efficiency.
  • Use the information to decide whether to adjust length, height, or configuration.

3. Which Tool Should I Use?

  • Doublet: One wire, many bands, tuner + ladder line.
  • Loop: Compact or small loop scenarios, noise rejection.
  • Skyloop: Large horizontal loop for multi-band coverage.
  • Vertical DX Designer: Low-angle radiation for DX.
  • Vertical NVIS Designer: Higher-angle radiation for regional coverage.
  • Performer Vertical: Squeezing performance from a single vertical in limited space.
  • Dominator Array: Multi-element vertical arrays for gain and directivity.
